# Baseline review — Checklist tab (before redesign)

Captured 2026-09-03 on iPhone 17 Pro simulator, iOS 26.4, dev build from `main` @ `3baa1412`.
Account: skilled worker, stage 4 ("3+ years"). Evidence in `.design/evidence/baseline/`.

## What the screen is today

- Header "Personalized Checklist", subtitle persona + stage, a flat grey progress bar at 0%, "+ Add".
- Four buckets in fixed order: Do now (4), Do soon (5), Explore and connect (5), Optional / later (3). 17 tasks, all unchecked.
- Each task: circle checkbox on a vertical connector line, a white card with the title clamped to 2 lines, a drag handle.
- Tap card → bottom sheet: bucket pill, full title, "Why:" paragraph, green "Mark as complete", "Learn how →" link.
- "+ Add" / "Add your own item" → full-screen form: Category radio (the same 4 buckets), Title, Short description.
- No dates anywhere. No section for "this week". No reminders. Nothing changes with time except the stage label.

## Live data behind it (Supabase, 2026-09-03)

- 147 rows in `personalized_checklist_tasks`; `show_after_days` / `show_before_days` / `month_start` / `month_end` exist and are NULL on all 147.
- 35 users hold 183 `user_tasks` rows, 50 completed. 7 custom tasks in total.
- PostHog, 90 days: 69 users switched to the tab; 6 users completed a task (17 completions).

## Rubric (native weights)

| Dimension | Weight | Score | Referent |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Task fit | 20 | 3 | Tasks are prompts ("Review your family sponsorship options", "Explore an event") not actions with a finish line. Nothing tells the user what to do *this week*. 6 of 69 visitors ever completed anything. |
| Content and hierarchy | 15 | 4 | Urgency buckets are the only structure, but "Do now" for a stage-4 user holds a citizenship application and a tax filing side by side with no dates. Titles clamp at 2 lines, so the actual instruction is cut ("…if you have met the 1,095-day phy…"). |
| System coherence | 15 | 7 | Tokens and components match the rest of the app: TabHeader, bottom sheet, tab bar, card radius, bucket colours. |
| Interaction and states | 20 | 5 | Complete, reorder, add custom, learn link all work. No overdue, due-soon, or done-for-now state. No empty state visible for a bucket. Progress bar at 0% carries no information. |
| Accessibility / responsive | 15 | 5 | Tap targets fine. Drag handles are small and unlabeled. Clamped titles hide content from everyone, worse at larger text sizes. |
| Visual expression | 5 | 5 | Calm and consistent, but indistinguishable from a generic to-do list. |
| Craft | 5 | 6 | Even spacing, aligned connector line, readable type. Progress bar and bucket icons feel like placeholders. |
| Evidence / fidelity | 5 | 7 | Matches what the code does; no divergence found. |

**Weighted: 4.7 / 10.** The limiting dimension is task fit. This is a structural problem: the surface has no concept of time, so it cannot answer "what do I need to do next, and by when".

## What must survive

- The bucket vocabulary (Do now / Do soon / Explore and connect / Optional) is in the data model, the personalize function, custom tasks, and i18n. Keep it as a facet, even if it stops being the primary grouping.
- The task sheet's "Why" paragraph and "Learn how" link are the best content on the screen.
- Custom tasks and reorder exist and have users (7 custom rows).
