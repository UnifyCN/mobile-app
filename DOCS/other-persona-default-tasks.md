# Default checklist tasks for the Other persona

This list is the approved baseline for users who select **Other** as their persona. Each task is useful without assuming a specific immigration pathway, school enrolment, profession, employment status, or family situation. The app lets users remove any suggested task that does not apply to them.

- `f9073a8e-57c5-46f8-a3b1-715d824058ff` — Organize your key immigration documents
- `a2d27bb4-180c-4946-9fef-6e631b4723e5` — Arrange temporary housing for your first 2–4 weeks
- `5e5a575f-cc4e-4e59-85d8-064f3cb9ca49` — Build a realistic first-month landing budget
- `70acce48-203a-4534-8657-80c64c5534b3` — Research BC's 3-month health coverage wait and plan for interim healthcare
- `1fdb4615-8da6-43a6-baf6-cd2dc0422b15` — Learn the most common rental and newcomer scams in Canada
